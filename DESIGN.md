## Overview

RS Waters uses a **premium hydrology** visual direction: clear, calm, precise, and product-led. The public site should feel like a credible modern water company rather than a generic SaaS landing page or a verification document.

The signature device is the **Hydroglass composition**: photographic water surfaces paired with restrained translucent panels, deep-water typography, and a small amount of aqua light. Use it most strongly in the homepage hero and sparingly elsewhere.

Avoid generic crypto/SaaS gradients, excessive floating pills, fake statistics, decorative counters, or claims that are not confirmed business facts.

## Colors

Deep ink and deep-water tones provide authority. Primary water blue is used for navigation and action emphasis. Aqua is a highlight rather than the dominant fill. Ice and canvas backgrounds keep the site bright and clean.

Do not use blue purely decoratively everywhere; leave generous white space and let product imagery carry visual interest.

## Typography

Outfit is the display face for strong headings and concise labels. Inter is the body and utility face.

Homepage display type can use tight tracking and compressed line-height at large sizes. Body copy should remain comfortably readable and restrained.

## Layout

The public site uses a wide editorial grid with generous horizontal breathing room. The homepage hero is intentionally asymmetric: text leads on the left, while the Hydroglass product/water composition creates depth on the right.

Sections should alternate between bright canvas, white product surfaces, and one deep-water business section. Avoid stacking many visually loud sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is subtle and selective. Product cards use faint low-contrast shadows only on hover. Hydroglass panels can use deeper soft shadows because they are the signature composition.

## Shapes

Controls use 12–14px radii. Product cards use approximately 24px radii. Large feature and hero media can use 28–40px radii. Avoid making every small label a pill.

## Components

Primary calls to action use deep-water ink with white text. Secondary actions use white or translucent surfaces with a visible border.

Product cards prioritize image, category, title, price, and one clear action. Business/legal identity stays visible in appropriate About, Contact, footer, and legal surfaces without dominating commercial marketing.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do make RS Waters feel like a real consumer and business water brand.
- Do use real product/category language and confirmed company contact details.
- Do preserve clear keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, and visible scrollbars.
- Do reserve image geometry to prevent layout shifts.
- Don't turn the homepage into a company-verification dossier.
- Don't invent certifications, customer counts, purity percentages, warranties, service guarantees, or delivery promises.
- Don't use decorative numbering unless it helps navigation or grouping.
